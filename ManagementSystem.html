    Internal Management System

    Small Franchise Coffee shops

    Creating internal management system in small franchise coffee shops to improve procurement processes that caused inefficient communication, low service quality and low working quality. Two plans are provided based on the requirements of fixing work schedules, clearly structured arrangements, and transparent communication between branch employees and management.
    After implementing plan B, we instantly improved communication processes, as the employees no longer needed to pay constant attention to their phones. With the reduction of phone time, the staff could instead focus on providing higher quality service which lead to improved customer satisfaction .

  • Solution
  • Result

    B. Cannot restock inventory immediately / correctly due to miscommunications, extra money spent on resources to allocate stocks from different branches, therein affecting inventory for other branches as well.
