    Customer Relationship Management System

    For hairdressers

    In the past, hairdressers relied on handwritten records to document customer services, hair conditions, and treatment details. While familiar, the process caused slow documentation, calculation errors, and longer customer waiting times. The project aimed to transform the workflow into a digital system that improves efficiency without disrupting existing habits.

    By digitizing the CRM system, we successfully reduced average searching time to 30 secondsand documentation time to 3 minutes, reduced calculation errors to almost zero, and improved staff workflow satisfaction.

    • Problem

    Hairdressers documented every service manually on paper sheets:

    • Difficult to retrieve historical customer records quickly
    • Time-consuming documentation during busy service hours
    • Customer waiting time increased while staff completed paperwork
    • Manual price calculation causes error rate 4%-5%
