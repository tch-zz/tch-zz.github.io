    EnglishCool

    Market Entry

    The “EnglishCool” is an English learning app for TOEIC preparation in Taiwan.  The company had planned to expand their market to South Korea, so this project aimed to find a market position by culture surveys and focus group interviews with potential target audiences.

    • Market Research

    According to the interviews and surveys, we found:

    []
    Korean people tended to choose more vibrant, colourful interfaces, with character representations or mascots as part of the theming. 


    []
    Preference for the use of ranking systems. It inspires and motivates users, due to Koreans generally having competitive natures.


    []
    Based on the analysis of the app downloads in Korea, the users prefer apps that provide personalised experiences, intuitive design, and localised connection.


    []
    Social buzz is also a metric for users to adopt and install an app on their phone.


    []
    Although the original interface of “EnglishCool” differentiated itself with a gamified style, from other English learning apps, Korean audiences prefer more structured interfaces that make them feel like they’re studying or preparing for a test.

    Target Audiences Analysis

    Age: 20-35
    Motivation: Enhancing job applications or achieve graduating standard
    Time: February and August are the peak times
    Preparation: 2 Months
    Behaviour/ habits:
    - Half of the candidates go to TOEIC classes, and the other half choose to join online courses
    - Intensively study at home without any social activities
    -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Opportunities:
    - Instant access to their learning progress and pointers on effectively improving upon their weaknesses are key in the short preparation timelines.
    - Using a character IP will also encourage the users, while giving them a personification of their motivation to keep moving forward with their learning progress.
