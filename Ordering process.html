    Ordering Process

    Franchise Coffee shops

    Improve customer circulation and reduce 30% wastage by streamlining customer ordering process, for a big franchise coffee shop located in Notting hill.

  • Problem
  • Solution
  • Result

    • Problem

    A. The main customers are tourists who are visiting the shop for the first time, and they lack familiarity with the ordering process and circulation.

    B. The seats in the ordering area block customer circulation and this causes added difficulty for the customers to reach the pick up point.

    C. Lids are available in the self-serve area, but most of the customers cannot distinguish between the size of the lids. There’s an unnecessary wastage of lids due to this.

    Solution

    A. Staff training: Import clear guidelines, divide customers into two groups: “Need more time to decide” and “Knowing what to order”. The staff who is the closest to the door is in charge of guiding the customers, making sure the crowd is flowing.

    B. Reduce the number of seats in ordering area down to 2-3 chairs, expanding the personal space per customer to minimise the chances of blocking pathways due to their personal belongings like suitcases, backpacks etc.

    C. Added signage in self-serve areas, displaying the different sizes of lids. Plus, rearranged the setup of the self-serve area to reduce the confusion.
